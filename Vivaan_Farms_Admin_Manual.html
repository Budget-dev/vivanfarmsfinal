Order Logistics & Tracking Guide

How to fulfill customer orders, assign tracking codes from your hand or via NimbusPost, update progress status, and broadcast live notes to customers.

1. The Dual-Fulfillment System Overview

Your store has been engineered with a fail-safe dual logistics architecture. You are never locked into any single courier platform. You can fulfill orders using either of the two operational modes:

Mode 1: Automated Courier

NimbusPost Automated Booking

Best for standard courier shipping via Delhivery, Blue Dart, DTDC, and Shadowfax. Automatically calculates rates, reserves waybills, generates barcode labels, and streams live GPS tracking.

  • 1-Click automatic AWB generation
  • Printable carrier barcode labels
  • Real-time automated scan sync
Mode 2: 100% Manual Control

Manual Dispatch from Admin's Hand

Use whenever NimbusPost is offline, or when shipping via India Post Speed Post, Farm Direct Vehicle, Local Tempo, Hand Delivery, or private transport.

  • Enter any custom tracking code from your hand
  • Post personal messages directly to the customer
  • Update location and progress status anytime

Section 2 Manual Dispatch & Customer Progress Notes

Step-by-Step: Fulfilling Orders from Admin's Hand

Whenever you want to dispatch orders manually or send progress updates directly to customers without relying on courier APIs, follow this 4-step process in the Admin Panel:

1
Open Order in Admin Panel

Go to Admin > Orders. Find the order in the list. Click either [Manual Dispatch] (for new orders) or [Update] (for orders already in transit).

2
Choose Courier & Tracking Code

Select your delivery method (e.g., Vivaan Farm Direct Express, India Post Speed Post, Local Vehicle / Hand Delivery, Tempo). Type your own tracking code or click Auto-Generate.

3
Select Progress Status & Current Location

Choose from the 6 official statuses: Processing, Farm Packed, Shipped, In Transit, Out for Delivery, or Delivered. Optionally add the location (e.g., Gujarat Vedic Farm Hub or Surat Transit Center).

4
Write Personal Customer Message (Or Click Presets)

Write a custom note in the message box. This note will immediately display on the customer's tracking screen! You can also click the quick presets below the box:

5. Frequently Asked Questions (Admin Cheatsheet)

Q: Can I update the message and status multiple times?

Yes, absolutely. Click [Update Note] on any order anytime. Every new status or message you save adds a new checkpoint to the customer's timeline without deleting past updates.

Q: What if NimbusPost is down or wallet balance is zero?

Do not worry. Simply click [Manual Dispatch], choose any carrier (e.g. Vivaan Farm Direct Express or Speed Post), and dispatch with your own tracking number. The website works 100% independently of NimbusPost.

Q: What if the customer gave the wrong phone number or address?

Click View Details & Invoice in the order row to review full recipient details. You can note the updated address in your customer message so the delivery driver sees it.

Q: How do I mark an order as Delivered?

Either click [Update Note] and choose Delivered (and optionally write “Delivered safely at your doorstep”), OR click the three dots (...) > Mark Delivered.
